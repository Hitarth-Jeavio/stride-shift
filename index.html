      Why this plan

      • Intervals, not non-stop running. Walk breaks let a beginner keep moving for 40 minutes instead of stopping after 10. For fat loss, total time moving matters more than speed.
      • Both work for fat loss. A 2019 meta-analysis found interval and continuous training each cut body fat by about 1.5 percentage points. Intervals did slightly better for total fat mass.
      • Run easy. You should be able to say a full sentence while running. If you can't, slow down. Walk briskly, not at a stroll.
      • Aim for 4 sessions a week. That's about 160 minutes, past the 150-minute weekly minimum. Weight loss usually needs 200–300 minutes a week, and food still matters most.
      • Climb slowly. Each level lengthens the runs and shortens the walks until you are running 10 minutes at a time. If a level felt hard, repeat it. Rest at least one day between sessions to protect your shins.

      Sources: Viana et al., BJSM 2019 · Run-walk ratios for beginners
